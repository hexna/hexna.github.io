What CardMaster does

  • FSRS spaced repetition — flashcards scheduled by the FSRS memory model, with review stats and per-deck progress.
  • FIRe skill trees — a fractional implicit repetition algorithm for systematic subjects (math, grammar, theory), where a tree's water level is the only thing that decays — so reviewing structured knowledge stays light.
  • RIA++ book distillation — turn a book or paper into structured notes, a mind map, and a dedicated AI skill you can then question in the built-in chat.
  • Reader + card making — epub / pdf / txt / md reader and a web browser with word-tap translation and one-tap card creation; OCR, audio and image-based cards for material you cannot copy.
  • Anki compatible — import .apkg decks; export decks (with progress) in CardMaster's own .cmdeck format.
  • 5 languages — English, 中文, Español, Русский, 日本語.

CardMaster is free and shows two non-intrusive banner ads. AI features use your own API key — the app has no account system and no server that stores your study data.

卡片大师能做什么

  • 基于 FSRS 的闪卡学习 — 由 FSRS 记忆模型安排复习时间,带复习统计与牌组进度。
  • 基于 FIRe 的技能树 — 面向数学、语法这类体系化知识;只有技能树的水位会衰减,结构化知识的复习量因此小得多。
  • RIA++ 拆书 — 把一本书或一篇论文拆成结构化笔记、思维导图,并生成专属 AI Skill,可在内置 AI 里直接提问。
  • 阅读器 + 制卡 — epub / pdf / txt / md 阅读器与网页浏览器,支持划词翻译与一键制卡;拿不到文字的素材可走 OCR 制卡、听力制卡、搜图制卡。
  • 兼容 Anki — 可导入 .apkg 牌组;导出支持 CardMaster 专属 .cmdeck 格式(带复习进度)。
  • 五种语言 — 中文、English、Español、Русский、日本語。

本应用免费使用,仅内置两个不会干扰学习的横幅广告。AI 功能需自备 API Key —— 软件没有账号系统,也没有存储你学习数据的服务器。
